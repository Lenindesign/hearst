Content reader

This spec covers the Hearst+ story reader: the modal shell that opens over the page, its sticky masthead, the context rail, the article card, the ad rail and the endless story queue. It gives each part's anatomy, the columns at each breakpoint, scroll and queue behavior, keyboard and focus rules, and tokens. Values come from the component source and from measuring the running app.

01 · Drawing HP-READ-001 · Rev A · Sheet 1

Anatomy

The reader is one dialog, named Story reader, portaled to the body. A scrim covers the page; the shell sits on it and is itself the scroll container. Inside, a sticky masthead sits over a grid of up to three columns. The full drawing is in reader-drawing.html.

1 · Scrim and shell

  • Scrim: fixed inset-0 z-[200], bg-foreground/55 with backdrop-blur-sm (8). A full-size click catcher under the shell closes the reader.
  • Shell: full viewport below 640 (h-[100dvh], no radius). From 640 it sits 24 inside every edge with radius 8 and shadow-2xl. It stops at 1360 and centers (from a 1408 viewport).
  • Scrolling: the shell scrolls (overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain, stable scrollbar gutter), not the page. It carries the brand's CSS variables and data-mode.

2 · Masthead

  • Box: sticky top-0 z-[110], background, 1 px bottom border.
  • Top row: 64 tall from 640 (padding 12 / 24), 69 below 640 (padding 12 / 16). Logo slot up to 230 wide; destinations or publications from 1024; round Close, 28 from 640 and 44 below.
  • Filter row: 46 tall. Topic filters hug their labels, 24 apart, centered from 640; below 640 they scroll sideways. The active one has a 2 px underline.

3 · Grid and rails

  • Outer grid: padding 16 / 24 (24 / 24 from 768, 48 / 24 from 1280), gap 16 across and 32 down. From 1280: 300 | 1fr, adding the context rail.
  • Inner grid (per story): from 1024, 1fr | 300, adding the ad rail.
  • Context rail: Trending, Saved and Suggested cards, padding 16, radius 8, 16 apart. Sticky at top 128, at most 100dvh − 160 tall, scrolls inside itself.
  • Ad rail: one 300 × 600 unit, radius 8, border 1: 268 of media over a body padded 24. Sticky at top 128.

4 · Article

  • Card: border 1, radius 8, padding 20 (28 from 640).
  • Lead media: 16:9, radius 4, opens the fullscreen gallery. Car stories use a gallery grid, video stories a 16:9 player.
  • Text column: max 768, centered. Kicker 11 / 16 bold, 12 above the headline. Headline T1: 32 / 36, and 48 / 52 from 768.
  • Action bar: 1 px rules above and below, padding 12, 24 from the headline and the body. Byline on the left; Ambient reader, Save and Comments on the right, 28 round, 8 apart (44 below 640, where the bar wraps to 146).
  • Body: 18 / 28, 24 between paragraphs; subheads 22 / 28 (28 / 32 from 768). Comments sit 32 below, padding 16 (20 from 640), radius 8.

Scroll and the story queue

Sticky parts

  • The masthead sticks at the top of the shell. Both rails stick 128 down and follow the story they belong to.
  • Back to top appears once the shell has scrolled more than 320. It sits 32 from the bottom and max(32, (100vw − 1360) ÷ 2 + 32) from the right; 44 round below 640, 28 from 640. It scrolls the shell smoothly to 0.

The queue

  • The reader opens with one story. A sentinel with a 600 px root margin mounts the next story when the end comes within 600, one at a time, until the queue runs out.
  • Stories are separated by an Up next rule: 40 above and below, hairlines either side of the kicker.

Which story is active

  • The reading anchor is min(0.45 × shell height, 420) from the top of the shell.
  • A story can win only with at least min(240, 0.35 × shell height) visible. The one that holds the anchor wins; otherwise the one whose top is nearest to it.
  • On a change the reader replaces the history entry with /read/{id} (no new back-button step), and the context rail and ad follow the new story.

04 · Accessibility

Keyboard and focus

MomentWhat happens
OpenThe page outside is isolated (use-modal-isolation). Focus moves to Close on the next frame.
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the reader and wraps from last to first and back.
Escape · scrim · CloseCloses the reader.
POpens the ambient (premium) reader for the story in view, unless focus is in a text field or a modifier is held.
Nested gallery or ambient readerThe reader stops handling Escape and Tab while the top layer owns focus.
After closingFocus returns to the live opener; if it is gone after a route change, to the element with the stored aria-label; for carousel stories, to the matching carousel dot. It retries every 50 ms for up to 3 s while the route remounts.

06 · For the design system team

Findings

These come from comparing the measurements with the source. Nothing has been changed in code.

F1 · LAYOUT

The article is narrowest at 1280

At 1280 both 300 rails are on, so the article is 504 wide with a 446 text column, narrower than at 768 (672) or 1024 (612). Here the measured 30-Day Challenge headline takes two lines at 48 px; longer headlines take three. Options: bring the context rail in from 1440 instead of 1280, or keep it at 1280 and use 48 → 24 grid padding there.

F2 · NEEDS A DECISION

Two different "story in view" rules

The URL and context rail use an anchor at min(0.45 × height, 420). The P shortcut picks its story with an anchor at min(180, 0.25 × height). Near a story boundary, P can open a different story from the one the URL shows. Fix: share one helper for both.

F3 · PHONE

The action bar wraps to 146 on phones

Below 640 the byline and the three 44 px actions don't fit on one line, so the bar is 146 tall at 390 and pushes the body down. Option: put the actions on their own row by design (byline above, actions in a fixed 44 row), so the height is predictable.
