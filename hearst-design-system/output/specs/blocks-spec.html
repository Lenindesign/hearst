HDS · Hearst+ building blocks · Spec

Blocks, cards and widgets

This spec covers the parts that make up Hearst+ home page sections: 8 layout patterns, 9 card types and 6 sidebar widgets from the Building Blocks Library. Each entry gives the part's anatomy, the tokens it uses, how it responds to width, and its states. Values come from the component source and from measuring the running app.

00 · How the parts combine

Composition model

Pattern = placement

A pattern sets where cards go, how many show, their proportions and the gaps between them. It says nothing about what a card contains.

Card type = content

A card type decides the fields and actions shown: an article, a video, a community thread, an event, an ad. Any card type can fill any pattern slot of a matching shape.

Widget = sidebar module

A widget is a self-contained module for a sidebar column. Widgets stack in any order inside a sidebar slot (for example, the side rail of Three-Column Feature).

In the library, every preview sits in a frame with rounded-[8px] bg-muted/60 and padding of p-3 · sm:p-5 · lg:p-7 (12 · 20 · 28). The frame is library chrome, not part of the block. Content width inside the frame (the "inner W" below) is the width the pattern actually gets.

02 · Card type 01 · Drawing HP-CARD-001 · Rev D

Story card

The Article card type is the production river card: LifestyleRiverCard in home-page.tsx, rendered with layoutOverride="blocks". Video, Make/Model/Year and Gallery cards are variations of it. The full dimensional drawing is in story-card-drawing.html. The film below walks through it part by part.

Anatomy (top to bottom)

  • Container: article, rounded-[8px], --hp-surface, --hp-shadow-card, overflow-hidden, border 0.
  • Media: fills W at 16:9 (aspect-video, object-cover). Its top corners are clipped by the container radius.
  • Meta row (.story-meta-row, z-20): 16px brand icon (r 3) · 6 gap · "Brand · Topic" (truncates) · "· Byline" (hidden below a 20rem container) · optional save button at the end. Text uses type-m1 (12/16, 600, +0.4px).
  • Headline: .headline type-t3 (20/24, then 24/28 from md 768), clamped to 3 lines, underlines on hover or focus. On vehicle stories a rating badge sits at the end of the row (gap 12, aligned to the bottom).
  • Summary: type-d2 (16/24, +0.2px), clamped to 2 lines, --muted-foreground.
  • Hit area: an invisible button covers the whole card (absolute inset-0, z-10). The meta row sits above it, so the brand link and save button get their own clicks.

Row and grid thresholds come from a 260px minimum card and the 16px layout gap (--layout-gap): 2·260 + 16 = 536, 3·260 + 32 = 812, 4·260 + 48 = 1088. PATTERN 01 · featured-four · mode featureFirstGrid

Featured + Row

A section heading, a full-width lead story, then a row of up to four cards.

Anatomy

  • Heading: headline text-4xl section title, with a 12px caps selection label at the end.
  • Lead: a standard story card (16:9, see Story card), then a 16px gap.
  • Row: .hp-block-row holding four standard cards, with a 16px gap (--layout-gap).

Responsive rule

  • The row follows the table above: 1, 2, 3 or 4 columns. With 3 columns the fourth card is hidden, so a row is never partial.
  • The lead stays full width at every size.

States

Row cards stretch to one height. Hover underlines the headline and scales the media 1.025; focus shows the inset ring; the bookmark save button sits in the meta row.

PATTERN 02 · four-card-row · mode cardGrid

Card Row

A section heading and a row of up to four compact cards.

Anatomy

  • .hp-block-row, 16px gap (--layout-gap).
  • Compact story cards (LifestyleRiverCard compact): 4:3 media, 12px padding, text-lg headline clamped to 3 lines, no summary.

Responsive rule

  • 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 columns at 536, 812 and 1088. At 3 columns the fourth card is hidden.

States

Same as Pattern 01.

PATTERN 03 · feature-sidebar · mode featureSidebar

Feature + Sidebar

A lead story beside a stack of four horizontal stories.

Anatomy

  • Grid with a 20px gap. The lead is a standard card.
  • The stack holds four list cards (layoutOverride="list", editorialRow, no summary or byline) at a 12px gap.

Responsive rule

  • Below a 820px block: one column, lead first.
  • From 820: columns minmax(0,1.8fr) | minmax(0,1fr). The stack becomes grid-rows-4 at full height, so the four cards together match the lead (stretchToFeature). Production: 615 | 341 at 976, and 800 | 444 at 1264.
PATTERN 04 · hero-three-horizontal · mode riverWithHero

River with Hero

A section heading, a full-width lead story, then three horizontal story cards.

Anatomy

  • Lead: a standard card. Then a 12px gap and three list cards (editorialRow), also 12px apart.
  • List card: grid 28% | 1fr, 4:3 media at r 6 (rounded-card-inner), padding p-4 · sm:p-5, headline lg · sm:xl clamped to 3 lines, summary clamped to 3.

Responsive rule

  • One column at every width. The media column is 28% of the card width, so card height grows with width.
PATTERN 05 · three-column-feature · library composition

Three-Column Feature Row

A discovery rail, a central feature and a contextual ad slot. It's built from production parts: TrendingStoryRail, a one-story EditorialHomepageSection and ContextualRiverAdvertisement.

Anatomy

  • Rail: Trending widget, 5 ranked rows.
  • Center: heading and standard lead card.
  • Ad: contextual river advertisement (16:9 creative, disclosure, CTA).

Responsive rule

  • Below 820: stacked (rail, feature, ad).
  • 820–1099: rail and ad stack in the left column (min 240) beside the feature, which spans both rows.
  • From 1100: .75fr | 1.6fr | .75fr (min 240 for the side columns). The center column never drops under ~480.
PATTERN 06 · featured-video-four · library composition

Featured Video + Four

A featured playable video, then four video cards.

Anatomy

  • Featured: LifestyleRiverCard featured. A video story renders the 16:9 poster with a 56px play button and a duration pill.
  • Then a 20px gap and .hp-block-grid with four video story cards.

Responsive rule

  • Grid: 1 → 2 → 4 columns at 536 and 1088. There's no 3-column step, so four cards always fill their rows.

States

poster → playing (inline AdaptiveVideo). Stories without a videoUrl show their poster with no play button.

PATTERN 07 · four-card-grid · mode grid (new)

Grid

Standard story cards in a grid that wraps.

Anatomy

  • .hp-block-grid, 16px gap (--layout-gap), any number of standard cards (the library shows 8).

Responsive rule

  • 1 → 2 → 4 columns at 536 and 1088. Rows stretch to their tallest card.
PATTERN 08 · horizontal-story-list · mode river (new)

River

A section heading and a stack of horizontal story cards.

Anatomy

  • List cards (editorialRow) at a 12px gap. Each has its meta row, headline and summary, plus the bookmark save button when saving is available.

Responsive rule

  • One column at every width. Media is 28% of the card width.

SOLUTION S1 · resolves F1 · F2 · F3 · F4 · F5 · DONE 2026-11-06

One card, one row rule

Today there are three implementations of the story card. There's LifestyleRiverCard and the library's StorySample. There's also an inline card inside the production Card Row (EditorialHomepageSection, cardGrid), with 4:3 media at r 6, p-3, a text-lg headline and no summary. The library shows patterns the home page doesn't actually ship.

Card

  • Make LifestyleRiverCard the only story card, with four variants: standard (today's card), compact (the row card: 4:3 media, 12 padding, no summary), horizontal (the list layout) and hero (overlay).
  • Make the library a thin wrapper that renders the real EditorialHomepageSection modes with sample stories, and delete StorySample.
  • Radius: add --radius-card: var(--hp-radius-card) to @theme so cards use the class rounded-card. Inner media uses a derived --hp-radius-inner: calc(var(--hp-radius-card) − 2px) (= 6). Codemod rounded-[8px] on hp-shadow-card surfaces (240 uses in src) and retire rounded-[7px] (8 uses, all in the library and one onboarding modal).
  • Height: cards in a row stretch to the tallest one, as the production grid already does (four cards at 1440 are all 357 tall). The article gets h-full, and content stays top-aligned with no pinned footer.

Row rule (container, not viewport)

Column count follows the row's own width, using a minimum card width of 260 and the 16px layout gap (--layout-gap; this rule first shipped with a 20 gap):

Row widthColumnsProduction check

Card Row keeps one line and hides the extra cards. Grid and Featured Video + Four wrap onto more rows. Feature + Sidebar goes side by side at ≥ 820 (production switches at lg, giving a 615 | 341 split). Three-Column uses three columns only at ≥ 1100. Between 820 and 1100 it drops to two (feature + rail, ad below), so the center column is never under ~480. The library's 2-up at 326 (157px cards) goes away.

F4 answer The 64rem threshold isn't a problem in production: the column is 1184–1264, so it gets 4 cards from a 1280 viewport. It only looked broken inside the narrower library frame. With S1, the library and production agree because both read the container width.
