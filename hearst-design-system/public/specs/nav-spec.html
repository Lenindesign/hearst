HDS · Hearst+ main navigation · Spec

Main navigation

This spec covers the Hearst+ header: the utility bar, the masthead, the section row, the compact nav that replaces it on scroll, the destination mega menu, the phone drawer and search. It gives each part's anatomy, sizes at each breakpoint, scroll behavior, keyboard and focus rules, and tokens. Values come from the component source and from measuring the running app.

01 · Drawing HP-NAV-001 · Rev A · Sheet 1

Anatomy

The header has three bands. Only the utility bar stays on screen. The masthead scrolls away. The section row scrolls away from 768 and up, where a compact copy takes over; below 768 it sticks under the bar. The full drawing is in nav-drawing.html.

1 · Utility bar

  • Box: sticky top-0 z-50 h-8 (32 at every width), inside PageContainer.
  • Grid: auto | minmax(0,1fr) | auto, gap 8 (12 from 640). From 1280: 1fr | auto | 1fr, so the pill centers on the page.
  • Weather: 72 wide (192 from 1024).
  • Destination pill: rounded-full, black at 10%. "All" sits outside the scroller; the other seven scroll inside the pill when it doesn't fit. A 1 × 16 divider sits before Local News. Links are 28 tall with 11 px text below 640, and 24 tall with 12 px text from 640; padding 4 / 8, gap 4. The active link is a white fill with semibold black text.
  • Account: hugs its label. "Sign in / Sign up" is 93 wide at 10 px below 640, and 116 at 12 px from 640. Signed in, it shows an avatar plus first name.

2 · Masthead

  • Banner (Hearst+ and destinations): full-bleed art at its own ratio from 640 (W ÷ 11.54), and a 5:1 crop below 640. The lockup is centered. On the Hearst+ home the banner starts under the bar (-mt-8 pt-8).
  • Logo row (brand pages): h-20 sm:h-24 (80 · 96) with the brand logo centered.
  • Controls: 12 below the bar on banners, vertically centered in the logo row. They are 44 × 44 below 640 and 28 × 28 from 640, radius 4, with a 14 icon. Left: the menu button (below 640) or the four layout toggles (from 640, gap 4). Right: theme and search, gap 8.

3 · Section row

  • Box: py-1 md:py-2 plus a 1 px bottom border. 41 tall below 768, 39 from 768.
  • Links: 14 / 20 text, horizontal padding 2, gap 24. Each link is at least 32 tall below 768 and 22 from 768. Active: semibold, --hp-section-title, 2 px border-primary underline. On Hearst+ a 1 × 16 divider follows Videos.
  • Below 768: left-aligned and horizontally scrollable, with 24 px hatched fades on whichever side has more links.
  • From 768: hugs its links (739 on Hearst+) and is centered.

03 · Drawing HP-NAV-001 · Sheet 2

Scroll behavior

768 and up · compact nav

A second copy of the section row is fixed top-8 z-40. It turns on when scrollY ≥ 96 and off when scrollY ≤ 64; between the two it keeps its state, so it never flickers. On: opacity 0 → 1 and translateY −8 → 0 over 250 ms, cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), and the original row is invisible. Off: aria-hidden and inert. Checked once per animation frame. Reduced motion: no transition.

Below 768 · sticky row

On destination pages the section row is sticky top-8 z-30 and stops under the utility bar. There is no compact copy (it is hidden below md).

Hearst+ home · bar fill

The bar is transparent over the banner at scrollY = 0 and fills with --hp-utility-bar as soon as scrollY > 0, with a 200 ms color transition.

Keeping the active topic in view (below 768)

On load, when the active topic changes and on resize, the row scrolls to max(0, previous.left − 16). If the active link's end still falls past scroll + width − 16, it scrolls to active.end − width + 16. The fades show when scrollLeft > 1 (left) and scrollLeft < max − 1 (right). A wheel gesture that is mostly horizontal (|dx| > 1.15·|dy|) is blocked, so trackpad swipes don't trigger browser back while the row holds its position.

04 · Mega menu · drawer · search

Menus and overlays

Destination mega menu

  • Opens when the pointer enters a destination link or keyboard focus lands on it. There is no caret. Click or Enter follows the link.
  • Closes when the pointer leaves the menu or the bar, on a press outside, on Escape, or when focus leaves the bar.
  • Placement: 8 below the bar (top-full pt-2). Width = min(Wmax, bar − 24). Left = clamp(trigger.left, 12, bar − width − 12).
  • Wmax: All and International 1152, Local News 1024, A&E Family 672, each other destination 384.
  • Surface: rounded-xl (11.2), border, shadow-2xl, padding 16 (20 from 640). Max height min(100dvh − 3rem, 680px), or min(100dvh − 3.5rem, 720px) from 640; scrolls inside.
  • Layer: absolute z-60, inside the bar's own z-50 layer.

Phone drawer (below 640)

  • Trigger: the 44 menu button, on destination and brand pages that have brand filters.
  • Surface: a modal dialog portaled to body, fixed inset-0 z-50, black scrim at 45%. The panel is min(91vw, 400px) wide (355 at 390), on the left, full height. It appears without a transition.
  • Contents: an 80 header with a 44 Close button; Browse Hearst+ (2-column tiles, 44 tall, gap 8, radius 8); Browse brands; Continue Reading; Join Groups; a footer with the theme action (44).

Search (every width)

  • A modal dialog portaled to body, fixed inset-0 z-50, black scrim at 70% plus backdrop-blur-sm.
  • The panel is inset 12 from each side and at most 672 wide, 64 from the top (96 from 640), with a 4 px border-primary top rule, radius 8 and max height 100dvh − 5rem.

07 · For the design system team

Findings

These come from comparing the measurements with the component metadata and the rule documents. Nothing has been changed in code.

F1 · DOC DRIFT

The utility bar is 32 tall on phones, not 44

utility-bar.metadata.ts says the mobile bar is 44 tall. The bar is h-8 sm:h-8, which measures 32 at 390, and its links are 28 tall. Fix: correct the caveat to "32 bar, 28 links below 640", and drop the redundant sm:h-8.

F2 · NEEDS A DECISION

Phone section links are 32 tall

Section links have min-h-8 (32) below 768, while masthead controls, drawer tiles and search controls are 44. The 24 px rule in APP_RULES covers utility controls only. Option: raise phone section links to min-h-11. The row would grow from 41 to 53 and the sticky header from 73 to 85.

F3 · LAYOUT

From 768 to 786 the section row runs into the gutters

From 768 the row stops scrolling (md:flex-none md:justify-center) and hugs its 739 px of links. At 768 the container's inner width is 720, so the row sits 9.5 px into each 24 px gutter (measured x = 14.5). It fits from 787. A longer brand nav would spill further, and with no fades and no scroll a link could be cut off. Fix: keep overflow-x-auto and min-w-0 from md, and center only when the links fit (for example justify-content: safe center).

F4 · DOC DRIFT

The home bar's navy doesn't match STYLE.md

STYLE.md says the bar fills with #012247. --hp-utility-bar points to --palette-alert-info-1000, which renders as rgb(3 17 43) (#03112B). Fix: decide which value is right, then update either the token or STYLE.md.

F5 · DOC DRIFT

Weather is in the utility bar, not the masthead

APP_RULES says the masthead's left slot shows local weather on tablet and desktop. In the app, weather is the utility bar's left cell (72, then 192 from 1024), and from 640 the masthead's left slot holds the layout toggle. Fix: update the APP_RULES line.
