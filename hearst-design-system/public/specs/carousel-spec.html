Featured story carousel

This spec covers the lead carousel used for Today’s Picks and for Featured stories on destinations and publications: the photo, the overlapping text card, the controls, the looping track, the parallax, autoplay, swipe, focus and the list layout. Values come from the component source and from measuring the running app.

00 · Walkthrough

How the carousel works

The film animates the carousel on a wireframe drawn from the measured geometry: its parts and sizes, the looping track, the parallax at a tenth of the speed, swipe, what pauses autoplay, focus, the list layout and one finding. It ends on the two drawing sheets.

01 · Drawing HP-CAR-001 · Rev A · Sheet 1

Anatomy

One article with aria-roledescription="carousel", labelled with the edition (“Today’s Picks”) or “Featured stories”. It fills its column. The full drawing is in carousel-drawing.html.

1 · Photo

  • aspect-[5/3], radius 8, fills the slide. The image sits in a layer 110% wide starting at −5%, which carries the parallax.
  • Hover zooms the image to 1.025 over 250 ms (off with reduced motion).

2 · Text card

  • Its color follows the page: the section banner on a section index, the brand color on a brand index (see Text card color). White or dark text, radius 8, shadow 0 6 18 rgba(0,0,0,.2).
  • Inset 6 from each side (18 from 640), pulled up over the photo by 40 (48 from 640), fixed height 224 (240 from 640), padding 10 / 20 (12 / 24 from 640).
  • Meta row: brand icon 20, brand name bold, optional Video badge, and a comments button (min height 32), 14 / 20 semibold, 6 above the headline.
  • Headline: T2, 24 / 28 (32 / 36 from 768), 800, clamp 3, max min(672, 100%). Car stories add a research rating on the right.
  • Summary: 14 / 17.7 (16 / 20.2 from 640), white at 85%, clamp 2, 12 below the headline.

3 · Controls

  • Top 20, inset 20, over the photo.
  • Edition pill: 24 tall, padding 4 / 10, 12 / 16 bold, black at 45% with blur. “Today’s Picks · 1 of 5”.
  • Previous · Pause · Next: round, 28, 6 apart, black at 45%, from 640. Below 640 only Pause shows, at 44.

4 · Track

  • Five stories plus a clone at each end (seven slides). The track moves by 100% per slide; the bottom padding of 24 leaves room for the card shadow.
  • Only the active slide is operable; the others and the clones are aria-hidden and inert.

5 · List layout

  • In the river’s list mode each slide is a row on a card surface: 112 | 1fr, 184 tall; from 640 38% | 1fr, 220 tall. Headline T3, clamp 3. The controls sit beside the photo.

01b · Context

Text card color

The text card has no fixed color. It inherits the color of the page it sits on, with the same logic as the masthead banners: on a section index it takes that section’s banner color, and on a brand’s index page it takes that brand’s color. So the same story card is red on the Lifestyle index, slate on Autos, and Esquire red on the Esquire page.

Where the color comes from

  1. Brand index page (/lifestyle/cosmopolitan/, /autos/motortrend/ …): the brand’s bar color, palette-background-utility from its tokens. Brands outside the token set (MotorTrend, Bring a Trailer, HOT ROD) use their supplementalBrandProfiles primary.
  2. Section index (Lifestyle, Autos, Fashion & Luxury, Enthusiast & Wellness): that section’s banner background in destinationBanners.
  3. Hearst+ home: the brand-wall banner navy, hearstPlusBrandWallBanner.
  4. Anywhere else (Newspapers, Hearst TV, A&E channel pages): black, the component default.

Keeping the text readable

  • If the brand’s own on-brand text color (palette-content-default-link) reaches 4.5:1 on its color, use it. Esquire gets black on its red.
  • If the color is light (white text below 3:1) and #121212 reaches 5.5:1, keep the color and use dark text (Good Housekeeping, Autoweek).
  • Otherwise use white text and darken the color just enough for 5.5:1 (Enthusiast & Wellness, MotorTrend).
  • On brand pages the brand icon sits on a small white tile, so a red icon stays visible on a red card.

Source: getFeaturedCardColors(destination, brandSlug) in src/lib/brand-banners.ts, passed to the carousel as cardColors. The list layout keeps the card surface.

03 · Drawing HP-CAR-001 · Sheet 2

Motion

One step

  • The track translates in 300 ms with cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1).
  • The card moves with the track and is offset by 10 px per slide of distance, so it leads.
  • The photo layer starts 16 ms late and runs 284 ms, shifted against the motion by 8% of the slide (7.27% of its 110% layer). During a drag it moves 8% of the drag the other way.

The loop

  • Next from story 5 slides onto the clone of story 1. When the transition ends the track snaps to the real story 1 with the transition off, and turns it back on two frames later. Previous from story 1 does the same through the clone of story 5.
  • Reduced motion: no transitions, no parallax, no autoplay, no hover zoom.

04 · Input

Swipe and autoplay

RuleValue
AutoplayOne slide every 6.5 s. The timer restarts on every slide change.
Pauses whileThe pointer is over the carousel, or a finger is down.
Pauses until ResumeFocus enters the carousel (except on the Pause button itself), or the reader presses Pause.
Never playsReduced motion; the button is disabled and says why.
Swipe (Today’s Picks only)Horizontal only (|dx| > 1.2 × |dy|, touch-pan-y). The slide follows up to 24% of its width. It moves on past min(64, 14% of W), or on a flick of 24 px at 0.45 px/ms or faster. The click after a swipe is suppressed.
TrackpadHorizontal wheel deltas add up; 55 moves one slide, then input locks for 520 ms. A pause longer than 180 ms or a change of direction resets the sum.

05 · Accessibility

Keyboard and focus

PartBehavior
Tab orderActive slide only: Open story (a full-slide button), Comments, then Previous, Pause, Next.
Other slidesaria-hidden and inert, so they are skipped and not announced.
StatusA live region reads “Story N of 5: title”. It is polite while paused or with reduced motion, and off while rotating.
Help textScreen readers hear: use the previous and next buttons (or swipe) to move between stories.
Arrow keysNot handled. Keyboard users move with Previous and Next.

07 · For the design system team

Findings

These come from comparing the measurements with the source and the metadata. Nothing has been changed in code.

F1 · BUG

The full-width hero headline overflows on phones

With scrimDirection="left-to-right" (the full-width hero layout) the headline keeps a 33% max width and loses its line clamp. That rule was for text over the photo; the text now sits in its own card. At 390 the headline is 102 wide, breaks words in the middle (“Collabo-ration”) and its 272 px of content overflows the 224 card. At 1024 it is 297 wide and four lines. Fix: drop the 33% cap and keep line-clamp-3, or apply the cap only from 1024.

F2 · PHONE

“Latest available” loses swipe, and phones lose every manual control

Swipe is on only when the label is exactly “Today’s Picks”. When the feed falls back to “Latest available”, or on Featured stories, swipe is off. Below 640 Previous and Next are hidden, so a phone reader can only wait for autoplay or pause it. Fix: enable swipe for every edition, or show Previous and Next at 44 on phones.

F3 · DOC DRIFT

The metadata lists scrim tokens the carousel no longer uses

featured-story-carousel.metadata.ts lists --hp-image-scrim-soft, -strong and -solid. The component no longer draws a scrim; the text sits in a black card, and scrimDirection only changes the headline width (see F1). Fix: update the metadata, and rename or remove the prop.
