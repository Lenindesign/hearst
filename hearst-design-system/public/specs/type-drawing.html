NOTES:(UNLESS OTHERWISE SPECIFIED)
  1. Do not scale from screen. Read the numbers.
  2. Today the app ships 42 line heights and 40 letter spacings as one-off classes. The HDS line-height and letter-spacing tokens exist but are not wired to CSS.
  3. Measured in Chromium on the home river and the reader at 390 and 1440 px (dashed values and open dots).
  4. Specimen text is placeholder. Metrics, not copy, are specified.
THE STANDARD:
  1. LH = 4 · round(FS · R ÷ 4). Text roles use the HDS ratio for their size; headlines use RH.
  2. Tracking = HDS token for the size. Caps labels +0.1em. Display (40+) −0.01em. No other values.
  3. Spacing between roles sits on the 4 px grid (S1–S9). Roles on one row share one line height.
  4. Kerning stays on (font-kerning: normal). Dates and counts use tabular figures.
